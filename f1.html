How a race weekend works

Two picks. Twenty-two drivers.

01

Order the grid

Put all 22 drivers in the order you think they’ll qualify. Locks when qualifying starts.

02

Order the finish

Start from the real grid and move who you think gains or drops. Locks at lights out.

03

Watch it live

The order and lap count update during the race, with your exactly-right picks ticked and your points so far.
