F1 help

Grid, race and sprint picks every race weekend, and the season predictions.

F1 picksF1 season predictions

01F1 picks

Every race weekend you put the whole field in order twice: the grid before qualifying and the finishing order before the race. Sprint weekends add the same two picks for the sprint.

  1. Switch to F1. Tap the sport chooser in the top bar.
  2. Open the weekend. Predict. The next race is chosen for you.
  3. Grid tab. Put all 22 drivers in the order you think they’ll qualify. Tap one driver, then another, to swap them, or use the arrows.
  4. Race tab. Put all 22 in your finishing order. Start from qualifying copies the real grid once it’s in.
  5. Save. Save each tab. Change it as often as you like until it locks.
Locks
The grid when qualifying starts, the race at lights out. Sprint picks lock at sprint qualifying and the sprint.
Grid points
5 for each driver in exactly the right place, all 22. The same for the sprint grid.
Race points
What that place pays in F1: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1, then 1 point for 11th to 22nd.
Sprint points
8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 for 1st to 8th.
Live
The order now, your exactly-right picks ticked, and your points if it finished that way.

Short on time? Start from championship order and move only the drivers you disagree with.

02F1 season predictions

The Drivers’ Champion is free in every F1 league. With Pro, your admin can add the full drivers’ and constructors’ tables.

  1. Open them. Predict → Season, in your F1 league.
  2. Choose your champion. Pick a driver and save.
  3. Order the tables. If your league has them, put the drivers and teams in order and save each.
Points
20 for the right champion and 20 for each table place exactly right, unless your admin changes them.
Deadline
Everything locks at your admin’s deadline, then shows how it stands against the real championship.
