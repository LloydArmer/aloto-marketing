Football help

Predictions, points, Triple Points, the final table and cups.

01Making your predictions

Predict the score of every fixture in the gameweek. Each fixture locks at its own kick-off, not at one weekly deadline.

  1. Open the gameweek. Predict → My Predictions. The current gameweek is shown first.
  2. Enter scores. Type a score for each fixture. Do some now and the rest later.
  3. Save. Tap Save. You can change any fixture until it kicks off.
  4. See how everyone did. Predict → Gameweek Results, once fixtures have kicked off.
Hidden until kick-off
Nobody sees your prediction before the fixture locks. Not other players, not your admin.
Missed one?
If Saturday’s early game has started, you can still predict Sunday’s.

02Points and bonuses

Points for the right result, more for the exact score, and bonuses for a perfect gameweek. Your admin sets the values, so check your competition’s Scoring for exact numbers.

Correct result
You called the win, draw or loss.
Exact score
Both scores right. Added on top of the result points.
Full house: results
Every result in the gameweek right. You must have predicted every fixture.
Full house: scores
Every score exactly right. Rarer, and worth more.
Voided fixture
A postponed fixture your admin voids counts for nothing and doesn’t spoil a full house.

A common setup is 2 points for the result and 3 more for the exact score. Yours may differ.

03Triple Points

A chip that triples everything you score in one football gameweek, bonuses included. You get two a season: one before 31 December and one after.

  1. Predict every fixture. The chip only unlocks once the whole gameweek is predicted.
  2. Play it. Predict → My Predictions. Tap Triple Points 1 or Triple Points 2 before the gameweek starts.
  3. Confirm. It can’t be undone, and it uses that half of the season’s chip.
Blocked gameweeks
Your admin can block Triple Points for a gameweek.
Use it or lose it
An unplayed chip scores nothing when its half of the season ends.

04Season predictions

Calls you make once, before the season, scored at the end. Everything stays sealed until the deadline, then everyone can compare.

  1. Open them. Predict → Season.
  2. Final league table. Choose a team for each place, from 1st to last. Clear all starts again.
  3. Season questions. Pick an answer for each one, such as league winner or Super Bowl winner.
  4. Beat the deadline. The countdown is on your Dashboard.

Season points go into a separate Season column, added to your total at the end of the season.

05Cup competitions

A knockout played alongside the league. You don’t predict separately: your normal gameweek predictions count for both.

How a tie works
You’re drawn against another player for a gameweek. Whoever scores more goes through.
Level scores
A replay in the next gameweek.
Group + Knockout
A round-robin group stage first, then the top players go into the bracket.

Follow the draw under Standings → Cup.

06Standings and live scores

Standings shows where you are while the games are on, with arrows for who is moving up and down.

  1. Overall. Standings → Overall for the season-long table.
  2. Monthly. Standings → Monthly, so somebody wins every month.
  3. Cup. Standings → Cup for the draw, ties and results.
  4. Season. Standings → Season for the season predictions.
Live points
Added as goals go in. They become final when the result is confirmed.
Lock screen
On iPhone, your live points and league position show on the lock screen while the gameweek is in play.
