Football in ALOTO

Pick the winner. Nail the margin.

Predict the score of every game. Points for the right winner, more for each team score you get exactly, more the closer your winning margin is, and the most for the exact score.

  • Every game locks at its own kick-off, Thursday night to Monday
  • Points that add up winner, each exact team score, and how close your margin is
  • Super Bowl, AFC and NFC champions free to predict in every NFL league
  • NFL reminders that open straight into your NFL league
NFL in ALOTO

Live on your lock screen

Your score, live points and league position update as the match goes on, without opening the app.

  • Live points as each goal goes in
  • Your position moving up and down the table
  • Your pick next to the real score
  • Reminders before your gameweek locks

The group chat, where it belongs

Every competition has its own chat, right next to the table it’s arguing about.

  • Replies and @mentions to keep the conversation going
  • Reactions for the exact score nobody saw coming
  • Admin moderation remove messages and ban from chat
  • Report and block for everyone

Cups that run themselves

Head-to-head ties decided on gameweek points. No extra predictions: your league form carries you through or knocks you out.

  • Knockout drawn on gameweek scores
  • Group + Knockout round-robin groups first
  • Replays when scores are level
  • The draw and results, all in Standings
