What it is

A private prediction league for your lot, and nobody else

One person sets a competition up and shares a six-character code. Everyone else joins with it, puts a score against every fixture, and the app scores it the moment results go in.

The table keeps itself

Results, exact scores, bonuses and Triple Points, recalculated the moment a score goes in. Monthly standings run alongside the overall table, so somebody wins something even when the title race is over.

Locked at kick-off

Each fixture closes at its own kick-off, so a Friday night game shuts while Saturday's are still open. Nobody's predictions are visible to anyone until the whistle.

No public leaderboards

There's no directory, no browsing, no global ranking. If you weren't sent the code, the competition doesn't exist as far as you're concerned.
