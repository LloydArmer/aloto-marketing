Why it exists

It started as a spreadsheet, the way these things always do

Every group has one. A tab per gameweek, a formula somebody wrote in 2019 that nobody dares touch, and one person doing the sums on a Sunday night while everyone else waits to find out whether they've gone up or down.

And every group has the other thing too — the argument. Somebody swears blind they said 2–1. There's no record. It gets settled by whoever shouts longest.

The app is just the spreadsheet, minus the Sunday night, minus the arguing.

Predictions are timestamped and locked at kick-off, so what you said is what you said. The maths happens the moment results go in. And because a competition is joined by code and shows up nowhere else, it stays what it always was: your lot, and nobody else.

Three ways to run it

A season, a cup, or both at once

Every competition is separate, and you can run as many as your group has appetite for.

League

The long game

Weekly gameweeks feeding a running points table, with a monthly winner declared alongside the overall standings. This is where your scoring rules live.

Knockout

Head to head

Players are drawn against each other and the higher gameweek score goes through. Level on points means a replay. Cup upsets are the whole point.

Group + Knockout

Tournament format

A round-robin group stage over several gameweeks, then the top players carry into a bracket. Best suited to a Euros or World Cup summer.

Before you start one

The things people ask first

Does everyone have to pay?

No. Players never pay anything, ever. Pro is bought once by whoever runs the competition, and it covers everyone in it.

Can people see my predictions before the game?

No. Every prediction is hidden until that fixture kicks off — from other players and from the admin. It's the thing the whole competition rests on.

What if I miss the deadline?

The fixture scores zero and you lose the full-house bonuses for that gameweek. Nobody can let you in late, including the admin. That's deliberate.

Can I change the scoring?

Yes. The 2 and 5 are defaults. Admins can set their own values for results, exact scores and both full-house bonuses.

What happens if a match is postponed?

The admin voids the fixture. It comes out of the scoring for everyone and doesn't cost anybody their full-house bonus.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — iPhone first. The web app works in any mobile browser in the meantime, and Android is on the list.

How many people can be in a competition?

As many as you like. It works just as well for six mates as for a workplace of sixty.
