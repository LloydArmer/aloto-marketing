Watch a season come together

Five steps from an empty screen to a settled argument

01

Create the competition

Pick a format, give it a name, and you become its admin. League competitions hold the points rules; cups and tournaments borrow theirs from a league you already run.

02

Share the join code

Every competition gets its own code. Paste it into the group chat and that is the whole invitation. Without it, nobody can find the competition, let alone join it.

03

Build the gameweek

Add the fixtures you want to cover and set the kick-off times. Each fixture locks at its own kick-off, so a Friday night game closes while Saturday's are still open.

04

Everyone predicts

Players put a score against every fixture. Predictions save as they go, and stay hidden from everyone else — the admin included — until the match starts.

05

The table takes shape

Enter the results and the maths happens on its own: points, full-house bonuses, Triple Points, the running table, the monthly winner, and any cup tie riding on that gameweek.

Three ways to run it

A season, a cup, or both at once

Every competition is separate, and you can run as many as your group has appetite for.

League

The long game

Weekly gameweeks feeding a running points table, with a monthly winner declared alongside the overall standings. This is where your scoring rules live.

Knockout

Head to head

Players are drawn against each other and the higher gameweek score goes through. Level on points means a replay. Cup upsets are the whole point.

Group + Knockout

Tournament format

A round-robin group stage over several gameweeks, then the top players carry into a bracket. Best suited to a Euros or World Cup summer.

Scoring

Two chips a season — one before New Year, one after. Triples everything you score that gameweek, as long as you've predicted every fixture.

Private by design

Your league, nobody else's business

Join by code only

There is no public directory and no browsing other people's competitions. If you weren't sent the code, you can't get in.

No global leaderboard

You're not ranked against strangers. The only table that exists is the one with your mates in it.

Picks hidden until kick-off

Nobody can see what anyone else has gone for until the match starts. No copying, no late tactical changes.
