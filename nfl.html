How a week works

Every game. Its own kick-off.

01

Enter both scores

The margin you’re calling shows underneath, for example “Chiefs by 7”.

02

Locks at kick-off

Each game locks on its own, from Thursday night through to Monday.

03

Overtime counts

Final scores include overtime. Calling a tie that ends tied counts as the right winner.
