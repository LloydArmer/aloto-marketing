Playing

01Getting started

ALOTO is invite-only. You join a competition with the six-character code your admin shares, and everything else is set up for you.

  1. Download. Get ALOTO Prediction Pro free from the App Store.
  2. Join. Dashboard → Join a competition. Enter the code and tap Join.
  3. Pick a competition. If you’re in more than one, choose it from the selector at the top of Predict or Standings.
  4. Switch sport. Playing football and NFL? Tap the sport chooser in the top bar.
  5. Turn on reminders. Settings → Push notifications, on each phone you use.

Got a second code? Settings → Join a competition works too. There’s no limit to how many competitions you can play in.

02Making your predictions

Predict the score of every fixture in the gameweek. Each fixture locks at its own kick-off, not at one weekly deadline.

  1. Open the gameweek. Predict → My Predictions. The current gameweek is shown first.
  2. Enter scores. Type a score for each fixture. Do some now and the rest later.
  3. Save. Tap Save. You can change any fixture until it kicks off.
  4. See how everyone did. Predict → Gameweek Results, once fixtures have kicked off.
Hidden until kick-off
Nobody sees your prediction before the fixture locks. Not other players, not your admin.
Missed one?
If Saturday’s early game has started, you can still predict Sunday’s.

03Points and bonuses

Points for the right result, more for the exact score, and bonuses for a perfect gameweek. Your admin sets the values, so check your competition’s Scoring for exact numbers.

Correct result
You called the win, draw or loss.
Exact score
Both scores right. Added on top of the result points.
Full house: results
Every result in the gameweek right. You must have predicted every fixture.
Full house: scores
Every score exactly right. Rarer, and worth more.
Voided fixture
A postponed fixture your admin voids counts for nothing and doesn’t spoil a full house.

A common setup is 2 points for the result and 3 more for the exact score. Yours may differ.

04Triple Points

A chip that triples everything you score in one football gameweek, bonuses included. You get two a season: one before 31 December and one after.

  1. Predict every fixture. The chip only unlocks once the whole gameweek is predicted.
  2. Play it. Predict → My Predictions. Tap Triple Points 1 or Triple Points 2 before the gameweek starts.
  3. Confirm. It can’t be undone, and it uses that half of the season’s chip.
Blocked gameweeks
Your admin can block Triple Points for a gameweek.
Use it or lose it
An unplayed chip scores nothing when its half of the season ends.

05Season predictions

Calls you make once, before the season, scored at the end. Everything stays sealed until the deadline, then everyone can compare.

  1. Open them. Predict → Season.
  2. Final league table. Drag every team into the order you think they’ll finish.
  3. Season questions. Pick an answer for each one, such as league winner or Super Bowl winner.
  4. Beat the deadline. The countdown is on your Dashboard.

Season points go into a separate Season column, added to your total at the end of the season.

06Cup competitions

A knockout played alongside the league. You don’t predict separately: your normal gameweek predictions count for both.

How a tie works
You’re drawn against another player for a gameweek. Whoever scores more goes through.
Level scores
A replay in the next gameweek.
Group + Knockout
A round-robin group stage first, then the top players go into the bracket.

Follow the draw under Standings → Cup.

07Reminders

Push reminders so you never miss a gameweek. Deadline reminders only go to people who still have something to predict.

  1. Switch them on. Settings → Push notifications, on each device.
  2. Tap a reminder. It opens the right competition and sport, ready to predict.
Gameweek open
When your admin opens a gameweek.
24 hours to go
Before the first kick-off, if you have predictions outstanding.
1 hour to go
The final reminder. Later fixtures stay open until their own kick-off.

Reminders arriving twice, or not at all? Settings → Reset notifications, and check notifications are allowed for ALOTO in your iPhone’s own Settings.

NFL

08NFL picks

Predict the score of every game each week. Points for the right winner, more the closer your winning margin is, and the most for the exact score.

  1. Switch to NFL. Tap the sport chooser in the top bar.
  2. Open the week. Predict → This week.
  3. Enter both scores. The margin you’re calling shows underneath, for example “Kansas City by 7”.
  4. Save. Each game locks at its own kick-off, from Thursday night to Monday.
Scoring
The winner, plus your closest of exact score, exact margin, within 3, within 7 or within 14. Your league’s values are shown above the games.
Ties and overtime
Calling a tie that ends tied counts as the right winner. Final scores include overtime.
No chips
Triple Points and full house bonuses are football only.

09NFL season questions

Three questions are free in every NFL league. Others are added by an admin with Pro.

  1. Open them. Predict → Season tab, in your NFL league.
  2. Make your picks. Choose a team for each question before the deadline.
Free in every league
Super Bowl Winner, AFC Champion and NFC Champion.
With Pro
NFL MVP, the division winners, and questions your admin writes.

Running a competition

10Setting up a competition

Whoever creates a competition is its admin. Admin is in the top bar on a tablet or computer, and the top-right menu on a phone.

  1. Choose the sport. Switch to football or NFL in the top bar first.
  2. Create it. Admin → Competitions. League, Knockout, or Group + Knockout.
  3. Set the points. Admin → Scoring.
  4. Invite people. Admin → Players. Share the six-character join code.

A cup borrows its scoring from a league you nominate, so set the league up first.

11Gameweeks and fixtures

Admin → Fixtures lists your gameweeks under Current, Upcoming and Done. Open one and everything for it is on three tabs.

  1. Add a gameweek. Tap + Gameweek and give it a label, such as 1, R16 or QF1.
  2. Fixtures tab. Add fixtures. Results come in automatically; you can still correct one.
  3. Predictions tab. See who has predicted and who hasn’t.
  4. Settings tab. The month it counts towards, Block Triple Points, full house bonuses, and sharing with other competitions.
  5. Make it current. ⋯ → Set as current. This opens it and sends the reminder.
Fixture ⋯ menu
Correct result, Void (postponed) or Restore, Delete fixture.

Void a postponed fixture rather than deleting it, so it doesn’t spoil anyone’s full house.

Questions

The ones people ask most

I’ve lost my join code

Ask your competition admin. It’s under Admin → Players and doesn’t change.

I missed a kick-off. Can the admin let me in late?

No. Once a fixture kicks off it’s locked for everybody, and that’s deliberate. A missed fixture scores zero.

My points look wrong

Check the result in the app against the real one first. If it’s right and the points still don’t add up, email us with the competition name and gameweek.

Is there an Android app?

It’s coming soon to Google Play. Your account, leagues and predictions will carry straight over.

Do players ever pay?

No. Playing is always free. Pro is bought by an admin and covers everything they run.

I bought Pro on a new phone and it isn’t showing

Settings → ALOTO Pro → Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Your personal data is removed within 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.

Is there any gambling?

No. There are no stakes, odds or prizes. Points are for bragging rights only.

Still stuck?

Email support@alotopredictionpro.co.uk. It helps to include the competition name, the gameweek, roughly when it happened, and a screenshot if you have one.
