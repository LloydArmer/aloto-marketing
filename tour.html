Features

Everything in ALOTO

Predictions, live scores, leagues, cups, season calls and chat, for football, the NFL and F1. Here’s how each part works.

Predictions

Call every score. Locked at kick-off.

Enter a score for every fixture in the gameweek. Each one locks at its own kick-off, so Friday night closes before Saturday does.

  • Saved as you go and editable until kick-off
  • Hidden from everyone until the fixture starts, admins included
  • Triple Points two chips a season to treble a gameweek
  • Full house bonuses for every result, or every score, right
Live scores

Every goal, every point.

Scores come in automatically. Your points, the table and the cup ties update while the games are still being played.

  • Live table with movement arrows
  • Lock screen live points and position on iPhone
  • Gameweek results see what everyone predicted once it locks
  • No admin work results score themselves
Leagues and cups

A table, a monthly winner, and a cup.

Run a season-long league, a knockout cup or a group stage into a bracket. Your normal predictions count for all of them.

  • Overall and monthly tables, so the season never goes dead
  • Knockout cups head-to-head on gameweek scores
  • Group + Knockout round-robin groups feeding a bracket
  • Your rules every points value is yours to set
Season predictions

Make the big calls in August.

Put every team in the order you think they’ll finish, and answer the season’s big questions. Everything stays sealed until the deadline.

  • Final league table points for every team in the right place
  • Season questions league winner, top scorer and your own
  • NFL Super Bowl, AFC and NFC champions free
  • Revealed together once the deadline passes
League chat

Where the arguments live.

Every competition has its own chat, with replies, reactions and @mentions, right next to the table.

  • Replies and @mentions
  • Admin moderation remove messages and ban from chat
  • Report and block available to everyone
  • Unread badge so you never miss a dig
NFL

Pick the winner. Nail the margin.

Predict the score of every NFL game. Points for the right winner and more the closer you get to the winning margin.

  • Points that add up winner 2, exact team score 5 each, margin up to 12
  • Each game locks at its own kick-off, Thursday to Monday
  • Free season questions Super Bowl, AFC and NFC champions
  • NFL reminders worded for the NFL, straight to your league
F1

Call the grid. Call the flag.

Every race weekend, put all 22 drivers in order twice: the grid before qualifying and the finish before lights out. Sprint weekends too, and the order updates live.

  • Grid points 5 for every driver in exactly the right place
  • Race points what the drivers get, 25 down to 1, and 1 each for 11th to 22nd
  • Sprint weekends sprint grid and sprint finish picks
  • Free season pick the Drivers’ Champion
