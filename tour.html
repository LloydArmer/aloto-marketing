The tour

Every screen, as it actually looks

No mock-ups and no marketing angles — these are straight captures of the app. It plays on its own; hover to stop it, or click the bars to jump.

app.alotopredictionpro.co.uk
01

Join with a code

Someone sends you six characters. That's the whole invitation — without it, nobody can find the competition at all.

02

Put your scores in

A prediction against every fixture, saved as you type. Each one locks at its own kick-off, so Friday night closes before Saturday does.

03

Nobody sees anything early

Every other player's picks stay behind a padlock until the whistle. Then the whole grid opens at once.

04

Your season at a glance

Rank, total points, exact scores, and what you still owe. Season predictions and deadlines sit right there too.

05

The table keeps itself

Results, exact scores, bonuses and Triple Points, recalculated the moment a score goes in. Your row is the highlighted one.

06

Somebody wins every month

Monthly standings run alongside the overall table, so the title race being over doesn't end the season.

07

And the cup runs too

Head-to-head ties decided on gameweek points. No extra predictions — your league form carries you through or knocks you out.
